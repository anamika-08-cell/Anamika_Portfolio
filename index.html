About Me

I am Anamika Pal. For me, sketching is a form of meditation; it sharpens my focus and teaches me to notice the finest details. Wall painting is where my heart finds its voice,a massive canvas where I can pour out my inner thoughts using a brush. My inspiration anchors itself in nature. Spending hours on my veranda among the chirping birds and quiet flowers is a meditative experience that sparked my journey of self-discovery. Nature has taught me my greatest lesson: just as every flower has its own unique quality, I have my own identity. A flower never stops spreading its fragrance out of fear of fading away, and I will never stop trying out of fear of falling or failing.
